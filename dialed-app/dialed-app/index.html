True your BC from a real drop

Shoot at a known distance, note the elevation that centered your group, and enter it here. Use a distance where your bullet is still supersonic. Check your velocity with a chronograph first, since velocity error also shifts the result.

Install and share

On iPhone, open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app. After the first visit it opens with no signal, and your saved loads stay on your own phone.

Drop table columns

Choose which columns appear in the table. Your choice is remembered in this browser. Distance is always shown.

Bullet data comes from the open ammolytics/projectiles dataset (MIT License), compiled from manufacturer specs in 2018, so newer bullets and revised BCs may be missing. Defaults are a 6.5 Creedmoor 140 gr Hornady ELD Match load. Wind uses the crosswind component only, with no head/tail effect. Wind 2 and 3 replace the earlier wind from their start distance outward. Use chronograph data and confirm with real drops at distance before trusting any computed come-ups.
